About me

I am a biomedical engineer and neuroimaging researcher working at the intersection of advanced MRI, machine learning and mental health. My research asks how complex brain and clinical data can support individual-level insights that are scientifically robust, clinically interpretable and genuinely useful.

At the Melbourne Brain Centre Imaging Unit, I contribute to multidisciplinary research spanning psychiatric neuroimaging, healthy ageing, exercise neuroscience and imaging informatics. I also serve as a National Imaging Facility Informatics Fellow, supporting reproducible analysis and research capability across the imaging community.

My longer-term aim is to help establish what information derived from neuroimaging represents, when it remains stable, for whom it generalises, and whether acting on it improves outcomes that matter.

Current work

  • Longitudinal Affinity Scores: extending individual clinical fingerprints from cross-sectional profiles to interpretable trajectories.
  • Clinical validation: working with clinicians to define focused applications for explainable precision mental health.
  • Reproducible neuroimaging: developing multimodal MRI workflows and quality-control practices for collaborative research.

Selected research programmes

Affinity Scores

Explainable AI · Precision psychiatry · Longitudinal modelling

An explainable framework that represents each person by how strongly they resemble clinically meaningful reference populations and identifies which features drive that resemblance. The programme extends from cross-sectional profiling to longitudinal trajectories and clinical decision support.

Leadership, supervision and service

I lead and contribute to interdisciplinary research in explainable NeuroAI and precision mental health, from method development through clinical validation. I supervise graduate researchers across biomedical engineering, neuroimaging and computational mental health, teach data science, and support the development of early- and mid-career researchers.

My scientific service spans imaging informatics, MRI research networks and academic community-building at institutional, national and international levels.
